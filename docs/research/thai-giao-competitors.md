# Thai Giao (prenatal story) competitor scan

This scan surveys five distinct prenatal-storytelling products across six public web and app-store surfaces—four Vietnamese market offerings plus one global iOS app—as of 2026-11-24. All observations were gathered from public vendor websites and app-store listings without paid subscriptions, account sign-ups, or user interviews.

## Per-competitor notes

### 1. Mamibabi
- URL: https://mamibabi.com.vn/intro/apptruyenthaigiao
- Secondary URL: https://mamibabi.com.vn/truyen-thai-giao/thang-9
- What it offers: A Vietnamese pregnancy-to-parenting super-app built around a comprehensive 280-day curriculum ("Thai giáo toàn diện 280 ngày" / "280 ngày, mỗi ngày 1 truyện"). Includes a daily "THAI GIÁO HÔM NAY" feed (15–30 min/day for the full activity program, with 5–10 min/day story guidance), month-by-month and week-by-week story libraries, professionally narrated audio stories, comics, prenatal music, 1:1 expert chat, AI personalisation by gestational week, and parenting content extending beyond birth. Vendor materials claim 350,000+ users; the Google Play listing reports 50K+ downloads and ~4.1/5 rating across 107 reviews.
- Strengths: Broadest Vietnamese curriculum and multimedia catalog; strong daily habit framing across all 280 days; week-aware AI personalisation and professional audio narration.
- Weaknesses / gaps: Stories are a single unit inside a busy, multi-activity super-app rather than a calm, standalone long-form reader. Sessions are split across many activity types instead of offering an unhurried 10–14 minute read-aloud literary story, and web libraries act primarily as funnels into the mobile app.

### 2. truyenthaigiao.vn
- URL: https://www.truyenthaigiao.vn/
- What it offers: A Vietnamese web directory indexing short prenatal stories by pregnancy month ("lọc theo tháng").
- Strengths: Open web access with quick month-based browsing and no app install barrier.
- Weaknesses / gaps: Stories are short, with no daily week-by-day structure, no reading-time indicator, no due-date mapping, no reading-mode controls, and no mark-as-read progress tracking.

### 3. truyenthaigiao.online
- URL: https://truyenthaigiao.online/
- What it offers: A Vietnamese daily story aggregator that collects and republishes short prenatal tales in a lightweight web reader.
- Strengths: Simple web access framed around daily reading without requiring a heavy super-app.
- Weaknesses / gaps: Thin reader experience with republished short tales, no personalisation to gestational week or due date, no editorial continuity across a week, and no verified progress persistence or reading comfort controls.

### 4. Haru — Prenatal Story Diary
- URL: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/haru-prenatal-story-diary/id6760256673
- What it offers: A global iOS app structured as a prenatal story diary delivering 280 personalised tales (~3 min/day) with the baby's name woven directly into the narrative.
- Strengths: Clear 280-day diary framing and an intimate emotional hook via baby-name personalisation; focused scope compared to super-apps.
- Weaknesses / gaps: iOS-only and aimed at a global English-language audience rather than Vietnamese families; personalisation is primarily name-based; stories are very short (~3 min/day), leaving little room for a calm, immersive bedtime read-aloud session.

### 5. POH Easy
- URL: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.poh&hl=en-US
- What it offers: A Vietnamese prenatal and early-education mobile app (`com.poh`) paired with courses and the companion content website `poh.vn`, which publishes 35+ short prenatal stories grouped by pregnancy month.
- Strengths: Combines structured prenatal and early-education courses with free month-organised web articles for discovery.
- Weaknesses / gaps: Web stories are short articles inside an ad-supported content ecosystem with a relatively small catalog (35+ stories rather than a full weekly/daily cadence), and no dedicated distraction-free long-form reader or local progress tracking.

### 6. Short-story web libraries & month-bundle pattern (Mamibabi Month 9 & POH web articles)
- URL: https://mamibabi.com.vn/truyen-thai-giao/thang-9
- Related listing: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.poh&hl=en-US
- What it offers: Across the Vietnamese market, web-accessible prenatal story collections—such as Mamibabi's Month 9 (weeks 36–40, 5–10 min/day guidance) landing page and POH's `poh.vn` month-by-month articles—serve mainly as topical SEO/content hubs or top-of-funnel previews for broader apps and courses.
- Strengths: Easy to discover via search and grouped intuitively by pregnancy month.
- Weaknesses / gaps: Nearly all web offerings default to short 1–3 minute articles or app-download funnels, lacking consistent multi-day narrative arcs, transparent reading times, accessible dark/large-text typography, deep-linked week/day URLs, or offline-friendly client-side read tracking.

## The gap we fill

- **Long-form read-aloud sessions (1,050–1,500 words, 10–14 min)** instead of the 1–3 minute short-story norm across Vietnamese web sites and global apps like Haru, designed as a calm bedtime ritual rather than a quick scroll.
- **Word-count-derived reading time** calculated transparently from each story's actual length (`Math.round(words / 105)`) and validated automatically, so parents know the exact commitment before starting.
- **Mark-as-read persistence** stored locally in the browser (`localStorage`), keeping daily progress across reloads without requiring an account.
- **Deep links (`?tuan=<week>&ngay=<day>`)** so any specific week and day story can be bookmarked or shared directly.
- **Dark and large-text reading modes** tailored for low-light evening reading and visual comfort.
- **On-device `SpeechSynthesis` narration** using the browser's built-in speech engine—zero paid TTS dependencies, zero heavy audio asset bundles, and gracefully hidden when unsupported.
- **Weekly themed arcs aligned with published medical content** inside the `ivf-journey` app, weaving gestational developmental notes consistent with the repository's existing clinical reference data (`src/data/ivfData.js`) into a focused IVF-journey companion rather than a sprawling super-app.
- **No ads, no account, no backend**—zero ad clutter, zero login friction, and full privacy on the user's device.

## Sourcing & honesty note

- All competitor data in this document was gathered on **2026-11-24** from public vendor websites, search snippets, and Google Play / Apple App Store listings (the six URLs cited above).
- No paid tiers were purchased, no private app flows were unlocked, and no users or vendors were interviewed.
- User counts, download tiers, and star ratings (such as Mamibabi's 350,000+ user claim and Google Play 50K+ downloads / ~4.1 rating across 107 reviews) are vendor- or store-reported figures and may change over time.
- Unknown attributes (such as unstated monetisation or persistence details) are explicitly marked **not stated** rather than inferred.
- This document is a qualitative landscape scan intended to position the `ivf-journey` prenatal reader, not a hands-on feature benchmark or clinical evaluation.
